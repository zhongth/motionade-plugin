# Remake a reference video for my brand

Make a film for my brand or product in the structure of the reference video below. Keep its cuts and timing, its beats and what each scene does, its rhythm, motion character and soundtrack, and tell it with my brand, my product and my words. Map each element of the reference to the one that plays its role for us rather than copying its layouts, and leave none of its brand, copy, logos or pictures on screen. Learn my brand and product from what I name below or from this folder's code, and claim nothing my product does not do. Ask me when the subject or the brand is unclear, and ask me where the film's pictures should come from. Before you build, show me the brand kit and the storyboard with its story, and wait for my word. Build it the way a motion designer would in After Effects, as an editable Motionade project, with the [motion-design skill](../motion-design/SKILL.md)'s [remake route](../motion-design/references/remake.md). Then tell me, scene by scene, where it follows the reference and where it departs on purpose, and which groups and controls I would adjust in Studio. Deliver an MP4 and the Studio link.

The reference video and anything else are what the person wrote with this command: $ARGUMENTS
