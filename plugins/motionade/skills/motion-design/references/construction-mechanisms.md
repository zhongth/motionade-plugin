# Construction mechanisms and their limits

Read when designing phased text, a layered demonstration, or movement carried through a cut. These are teaching cases from studied constructions. They require no template collection or source-file import. Choose a mechanism because it supports this brief, then adapt its relationships to the actual content.

## Acquisition, reading, and release can have different clocks

In the SaaS Promo Replica study, text acquired its layout quickly while a long deceleration tail remained. Other properties controlled recognition and departure. For one phrase, word movement used a tighter stagger than deblurring, while whole-phrase scaling had already begun with very little early travel. Concurrent tracks therefore produced a perceptible order.

For a similar task, decide separately when the content reaches a useful position, becomes recognizable, receives reading attention, and visibly departs. Fit the property clocks to those events. Do not automatically give position, opacity, blur, and scale identical start times, durations, and curves. Identical clocks can still be appropriate for a single coherent gesture.

The source's fast-arrival curve `(0,0,0,1)` had about 89% progress at half the time; its departure curve `(1,0,1,1)` had about 11%. Those measured examples explain unequal speed distribution. They are not mandatory easing values. Distance, screen scale, duration, and coupled properties still determine the result.

**Failure and repair:** the last word becomes recognizable just as the parent scale or blur removes readability. Inspect the last word's combined rendered state and reading opportunity. Adjust the relevant property phase, stagger, or release; extending an almost invisible tail will not restore reading.

**Adaptation check:** change phrase length. Recompute the complete group span and reading opportunity, including the parent's motion. Separate entry and exit stagger density when the intended acquisition and release have different roles.

## A stable parent can host active content

The SaaS card demonstration held its outer composition while an inner deck advanced, then returned motion to the outer composition for departure. The stable interval provided viewing conditions for active content.

For a layered demonstration, decide which level supplies the stable reference and which level advances the information. Preserve rigid attachment and coordinate ownership while allowing internal state changes. The parent and content can exchange motion responsibilities through a phrase.

**Failure and repair:** every section performs an identical whole-panel arrival, hold, and reset, even though the information could continue inside a stable composition. Reconsider the hierarchy and sequence of responses. Repeated demonstrations may be appropriate when the brief calls for a deliberate comparison; variation is not an automatic improvement.

**Adaptation check:** add a feature or change its density. Reallocate exposure and internal movement while preserving the shared visual language. A miniature scene pasted into the previous layout may require a new composition rather than a shorter hold.

## Visibility selects which part of a movement the viewer sees

The SaaS source included movement that began before a layer became visible, and layers that stopped displaying before a movement fully settled. Many joins were cuts. Source key ranges, visible ranges, and scene boundaries therefore described different aspects of the construction.

For a movement carried through a cut or reveal, decide the pose and visible speed at first and last exposure. Inspect screen-space direction, scale, crop, and focus on both sides. If pre-running or clipping a movement, express it through supported native timing and visibility semantics, then verify playback. Preserve source clock distinctions when reconstructing.

**Failure and repair:** the outgoing movement visually stops, an empty interval appears, and the next object starts from rest, despite overlapping block ranges. Inspect first meaningful incoming activity and last substantial outgoing travel. Rephase or redesign the connection if continuity is intended. A deliberate cut or pause may serve a different purpose.

**Adaptation check:** alter the layout or visible travel distance. Same-direction movement alone does not guarantee comparable speed or a successful focal handoff.

## A transition surface can finish before the receiving phrase

In the Business Essentials reconstruction, a circle covered a light field while small dots remained independently visible above it. The receiving words were scheduled to arrive as the dots departed, but the enlarged foreground circle covered them. The repair ended the circle when its coverage had passed to the black canvas, then coordinated the dots' release with the incoming words. The user accepted the resulting reconstruction as an editable foundation.

The reusable decision is who owns travel, coverage, and incoming information. Inspect when the receiving composition can carry the background and what remains visible above it. Preserve independent motion where the subjects have different jobs. A persistent frame, mask, or camera matte may need to stay throughout the receiving scene; ending every transition object early would break that construction.

**Adaptation check:** change the receiving background or arrival time. Verify coverage through the transition, the first meaningful incoming cue, and the outgoing motif's release. Moving the incoming keys alone may leave an occlusion unchanged. Exact circle geometry, colors, dot count, and source frame numbers are case choices, with broader transfer still to test.

## Coherence lives across the sequence

The user's accepted source analyses pointed to recurring relationships across a film: visual language, information density, movement character, scale contrast, and attention transfer. Content replacement can preserve much of that organization, but changed copy, geometry, or meaning may require reconstruction.

Review complete phrases and joins as well as local motion. Use stable visual and motion choices where they support identity; vary event density, scale, or rest according to the content. Background movement is not evidence that information is progressing. A quiet result or end card can provide a useful viewing interval.

The earlier Orbit trial provides a failure case: a convenient helper applied one arrival curve to most transitions, and numerical overlap occurred after almost all visible travel had ended. The guidance had been read without changing enough concrete decisions. Inspect the authored choices and playback together. Do not introduce a curve-variety quota; repeated curves are acceptable when their role and observed behavior fit.

## Editable construction can still miss the visual idea

The original Vibe DJ film retained a native console rig and a visible A-to-B mix. It passed delivery and manual edit/Undo checks. The user nevertheless judged it a substantial quality drop from the reconstruction: repeated title, description, and illustration compositions felt like a presentation, with insufficient momentum.

The saved project confirms that three consecutive setting sections share title/subcopy positions and equal 115-frame durations. This locates a specific design pattern; neither repetition nor that duration is inherently defective. Here it conflicted with the requested fast, playful film. Preserving the rig and changing the background supplied consistency without enough composition development.

**Candidate repair:** use [motion composition](motion-composition.md) to test the visual premise, copy economy, connected key moments, and main handoff before detailing the console and scenery. Keep a stable product demonstration where it proves something; reconsider how the surrounding sequence develops. Faster versions of the same generic layouts would not establish a successful repair.

**Evidence boundary:** the user's verdict supersedes a sampled review's permissive creative recommendation. It does not invalidate native editability/export checks. Fixing Scene organization is a separate product-usability task, not proof of improved visual quality.

These mechanisms were distilled from source analysis, recorded repairs, and user critique. They remain candidates for transfer to different content and should not become universal recipes.
